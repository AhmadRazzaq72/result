Privacy Policy

Effective date: February 18, 2026

Stadium Route ("we", "us", or "the App") is a free mobile application that provides FIFA World Cup 2026 match schedules, stadium guides, hotel recommendations, and travel tools. Your privacy is important to us.

1. Information We Collect

The App is designed to work offline-first. We collect and store the absolute minimum data necessary:

  • Device location (optional): Used only when you open the stadium map to show your position relative to the stadium. Location data is processed on-device and is never sent to our servers.
  • Local preferences: Your favourite matches, match reminders, theme choice, and notification preferences are stored exclusively on your device using AsyncStorage. We never access or upload this data.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect personal information such as your name, email, phone number, or payment details.
  • We do not use analytics SDKs, crash-reporting services, or tracking pixels.
  • We do not create user accounts or require sign-in.

3. Third-Party Services

The App contains outbound links to third-party hotel booking platforms (Booking.com, Vrbo, Hostelworld) and travel-insurance providers. When you tap an affiliate link, you leave the App and are subject to that service's own privacy policy. We may earn a commission from bookings made through these links at no extra cost to you.

Map tiles are fetched from OpenStreetMap tile servers. Your device IP address may be visible to those servers when loading map data.

4. Data Storage & Security

All user data is stored locally on your device. Match and stadium data is bundled inside the App and optionally refreshed from our public GitHub repository over HTTPS. No personal data is transmitted during this sync.

5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Because we collect no personal data from any user, there is no special risk to minors.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by the "Effective date" at the top of this page.
